Open a story you already love, or pick three tropes. Kindred finds other stories that share that DNA — even when they come from a different format.
Kindred Score
Every recommendation gets a 1–100% match based on how much trope DNA the two stories share. Reader votes then nudge that score up or down — so the crowd can refine the ranking.
